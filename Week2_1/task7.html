Egg Curry Recipe

What you need

  • 2 eggs
  • 1 full onion
  • 3 medium sized tomatoes
  • spices
  • 2 potatoes
  • red chilli paste

steps

  1. Boil the eggs and potatoes inside a pressure cooker
  2. peel the boiled egg and potatoes
  3. smash the boiled potatoes and put the pan on the stove
  4. fry both boiled eggs, until the outer covering turns crispy and take them out
  5. put some oil on the pan, and chop the onion and tomatoes till the oil heats up
    6. fry the chopped onions until golden brown and add the tomatoes
  6. now, add all remaining spices and cook the gravy well
  7. then add the smashed potatoes, 1.5 cups of water and let it boil
  8. at last add the eggs to the curry
  9. Congratulations your egg curry is ready to be served
