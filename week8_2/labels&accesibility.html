Labels & Accessibility

Learn how HTML labels improve accessibility by connecting text descriptions with form controls. Explore two different methods of associating labels with input elements.

METHOD 01

Uses the for attribute to connect a label with an input through its unique id.

METHOD 02 Implicit Label Association

The input is placed inside the label, so clicking the associated text also activates the checkbox.
